A guest lecture for third-year psychology and first/second-year social science students on identity through an interdisciplinary lens: structural and sociological theories of identity, personality and the edges of identity (such as borderline personality disorder), and why a stable self-image matters. Included early findings from my research on gender identity — and the challenge of teaching the gender ≠ sex distinction in Danish, which uses one word for both.
