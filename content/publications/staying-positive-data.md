The dataset and materials behind my BSc Sociology thesis at the University of Amsterdam, which surveyed young adults (18–35) living with HIV in the Randstad about depression, stigma, health literacy and socioeconomic position.

The findings were published in 2026 as [*Positief blijven*](/publications/positief-blijven/) in *Mens & Maatschappij*.
