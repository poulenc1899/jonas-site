The EU-funded **IHI IMPROVE** project is building a platform for the smart use of patient-generated health data (PGHD) in care for chronic disease.

This data paper describes the dataset produced by IMPROVE's large-scale systematic literature search on PGHD in five disease areas: cardiovascular disease, chronic inflammation, neurology, oncology and ophthalmology. It contains metadata for **12,473 studies** (title, abstract, DOI) together with the screening team's inclusion decisions, in Excel and CSV formats.

The dataset can be reused to understand how PGHD is used in healthcare — and as a benchmark for developing and testing AI-assisted systematic review tools.
