Early warning signals (EWS) show up across very different fields — from genetics and photonics to veterinary science, climate and One Health — but each discipline tends to study them on its own.

This position paper came out of **Better Wave than Worry**, a project at UMC Utrecht that I coordinated as student lead. We brought researchers from across Utrecht, Wageningen and Rotterdam together to compare how their fields detect, interpret and respond to early warning signals, and what it would take to turn those signals into actionable outcomes.

The aim: an interdisciplinary community that strengthens science's ability to navigate the known and unknown challenges ahead.
